Isometric island in open ocean, new every load. Drag to pan, scroll or +/− to zoom, click a creature to inspect. Emergent, no fitness function; reproduction is mating-only. Bodies are solid and cannot stack. Body plan is evolvable — elongation, neck and leg length each buy something and cost something — so silhouettes drift toward serpents, waders, browsers and shark forms. A body commits to one medium: fins and wings draw on the same budget, so nothing both swims and flies. Two larders are locked behind anatomy: sea-floor growth (2.4x) needs real fins, and alpine growth (up to 3.2x) sits above climbable ground — you may only step up one block, so the summit belongs to whatever evolves wings.
